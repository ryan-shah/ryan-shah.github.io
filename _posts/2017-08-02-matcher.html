I did this project in order to solve a problem I noticed with my friends involved with greek life. When matching new members (littles) with their mentors (bigs), a few people had to go through over 100 different lists of member preferences and do their best to pair these groups of people.

I knew that there had to be a better way as this method normally took several hours and often caused mistakes. I created BigLittleMatcher to be easy to use solution that could ease this burden for many years to come, and worked directly with the people who would use it in order for it to perfectly suit their needs.

For a more detailed examination of the project, please view the source code.
