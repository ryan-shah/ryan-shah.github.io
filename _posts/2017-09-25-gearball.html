This was a project done at the beginning of my senior year for Introduction to Artificial Intelligence.

In it I created a computer model of a gearball, a more complex version of a rubix cube, and developed a program to solve it.

To do this, I used the A-Star algorigthm to find the optimal steps to solve the puzzle in as few moves as possible.
