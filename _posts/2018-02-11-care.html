This project was done as part of the DerbyHacks 3 Hackathon and won First Place!

I worked on a team of my friends in order to design a tool that helped paitents remember to do things like take their medicine.

Additionally, paitents could use the service to contact a nurse if they needed help or in the case of an emergency.

This tool was implemented through the creation of an Alexa Skill that could be run on a paitents Amazon Echo and interfaced with verbally.

On the nurses side, they could interface with our website in order to create notifications for their paitents and update their medical information.

For more information, please view the project source.
