This project was done as part of the DerbyHacks 2 Hackathon and won a prize for Most Potential Value for the World!

I worked with one of my friends to create a proof of concept smart home built with Raspberry Pi's

The goal was to modify a Barbie Dream House with these devices to show that controlling things like locks and lights could be done for much cheaper than devices on the market today.

We were able to create a web application that allowed the user to do the following:

  • Live stream a video feed of their front door
  • Lock and unlock the front door
  • Toggle lights throughout the house
  • Move the house's elevetor up and down

For more information, please view the project source.
